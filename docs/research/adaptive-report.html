Cricket strategy generalization study

Measured local experiment: 5 training seeds per condition, 16,384 requested PPO steps per seed, 100 evaluation innings per scenario and policy. Single-style and mixed-style agents share all hyperparameters and train on the standard surface. Held-out pitch conditions test transfer without pitch training. The static-control scenario disables adaptation; it is an ablation, not an additional held-out opponent.

PPO intervals use Student-t uncertainty over training-seed mean rewards. Baseline intervals are normal approximations over evaluation episodes. These estimate different uncertainty sources and must not be treated as a significance test. A zero training-seed interval can arise when every trained agent repeats the same action; it does not mean evaluation uncertainty is zero. Fixed evaluation seeds are shared across policies; delivery noise diverges with action choices. The reward is 8 × wickets − runs. All results concern this uncalibrated simulator; no real-world or SOTA claim follows.

Inspect study.json for episode-level data, truncations, complete configuration, source hashes, and package versions. Each checkpoint has a training monitor CSV and metadata. The limited training budget is a pilot experiment, not a convergence claim.
